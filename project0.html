Read Me

Every camera makes two decisions at once: how far it stands from the world, and how much it magnifies what it sees. Change them together and a photo can stay the same size but tell a completely different story about depth. The three experiments below isolate that relationship — first on a face, then on a building, then in one continuous move known as the dolly zoom.
